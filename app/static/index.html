      Reading aloud

      A message turns into speech only when it is played: by its Listen button, or by itself for the boxes, projects, computers and services you choose. Its project, who writes to whom and its subject are said first, then its content. The audio is kept to play it again, seek in it or change its speed.

      Voices in the cloud

      Voxtral TTS (console.mistral.ai, API keys). Kept in this computer's keyring, never shown again.

      Gradium (gradium.ai, API keys). Kept in this computer's keyring, never shown again.

      Local engine, offline

      Piper, installed by the console into a private Python environment of this computer (about 300 MB, then about 60 MB per voice): it runs on the processor, without network or account. Each voice is a model of its own: download the ones you want.

      How it reads

      Voice engine used

      Language: Auto guesses French or English from each message (its voice and the words said first follow). Past this length, the rest of the message is said to be skipped (6,000 characters, about six minutes, by default). Code blocks are skipped, links are read as their site.

      On by itself once the first voice is set up. Shortcut in an open message: p (play, pause).

      Questions, recaps, replies to you. A message read by itself shows a notification with Pause and Stop, and the desktop's media controls show it (as for a music player).

      Read aloud by itself

        For a box, a project, a computer or a service: its ⋮ menu in the tree, “Read aloud what reaches it”. Each one shows here, to turn it off without looking for it.

        Audio kept

        An audio file goes this long after it was last played (30 days by default). Past the size (300 MB by default), the least recently played go first, the one playing excepted. Applied at once.

        When a session's work is done

        A session that finished its work sends its recap to this console, where it stays until you delete it. Then, for every session of this computer (the butler always keeps listening):

        What a session does after its recap

        Window

        On (the default): the system's title bar is removed and the app bar takes its place, with its own buttons to minimize, maximize or restore, and close; drag the app bar to move the window, double-click it to maximize or restore. Off: the system's title bar. Applied at once.

        Debug

        Off: the list keeps only what is useful: the messages still waiting to be read, those waiting for your decision, and everything written to this console (questions, recaps), which stays until you delete it. A message leaves the list once a session took it. On: every message seen stays, consumed ones included.

        Purge

        The purge (Memory view) removes from this computer every message that no longer waits in a box: the boxes' logs, their attached files, the console's copy. What an open session sent, received or saw within this delay stays, so that it can still answer it. 24 hours by default.

        Questions from sessions

        A session whose messages are in "Ask" mode puts its question here, with the solutions it proposes; your answer goes back to its box.

        Signal when a question arrives

        Red dot: on the console's icon in the notification area (and on the tab). "None" turns off the dot and the sound.

        Sound played

        Played by the console's open tab, otherwise by this computer. The ten sounds offered are royalty-free (generated for the console).

        A broadcast is information or a question, never a task: every listening session reads it and may analyse it. Keep it for what really concerns everyone.
